# Operations Research and Management Science (minor)

- Catalog: https://catalog.cornell.edu/programs/operations-research-management-science-minor/ (2026-2027 edition)
- Data file: `src/data/minors/orms.ts`
- Checked on 2026-11-13 by `claude-opus-5-5`
- Verdict: **out-of-date**

The core requirement matches the catalog: at least three of ENGRD 2700, ORIE 3300, 3310, 3500, 3510 and 4580. The elective requirement has two problems. First, its checker rejects the core ORIE courses, but the catalog lets a fourth core course count as one of the three electives. The description shown to students says the opposite of what the checker does. Second, the file omits the footnote capping ORIE 4900, 4999 and 4990 combined at three credits. It also counts courses only and never checks the 18-credit minimum.

## Findings

### 1. OR/MS Minor Requirement 2 (high, course-list)

- **Catalog:** Elective Courses: "Three additional courses chosen from either the core courses above, or any ORIE courses at the 3000 level or higher."
- **CoursePlan:** The checker returns false for ORIE 3300, ORIE 3310, ORIE 3500, ORIE 3510 and ORIE 4580. It also rejects STSCI 3510, which is cross-listed with ORIE 3510. ENGRD 2700 is rejected because it is not an ORIE course. A student who takes a fourth core course therefore cannot count it as an elective.
- **Suggested change:** Remove the exclusion of core courses. Accept ENGRD 2700 and the core ORIE courses (plus cross-listings) in the elective checker, alongside any ORIE 3000+ course. The two requirements cannot double count a course anyway, so the exclusion is not needed.

### 2. OR/MS Minor Requirement 2 (medium, rule)

- **Catalog:** [fn1] "At most, three of these credits can be satisfied by ORIE 4900 Undergraduate Research in ORIE, ORIE 4999 ORIE Project, and ORIE 4990 Teaching in ORIE combined."
- **CoursePlan:** There is no limit. ORIE 4900, ORIE 4990 and ORIE 4999 all match the ORIE 3000+ checker, so all three elective slots could be filled with them.
- **Suggested change:** Add a checkerWarning, or a separate check, that limits ORIE 4900, ORIE 4990 and ORIE 4999 combined to at most 3 credits toward the electives. Mention the limit in the description.

### 3. OR/MS Minor Requirement 2 (low, description)

- **Catalog:** Electives may be chosen from the core courses or any ORIE course at the 3000 level or higher.
- **CoursePlan:** The description reads "Any ORIE courses at the 3000 level or higher including those above," but the checker excludes the core courses listed above.
- **Suggested change:** Make the description and checker agree: fix the checker as above and keep the description saying core courses may count.

### 4. Minimum credits for minor (low, count-or-credits)

- **Catalog:** "Minimum Credits for Minor: 18" and "satisfactory completion of six courses (at least 18 credits)"
- **CoursePlan:** Both requirements count courses (3 + 3). Total credits are never checked, so six low-credit ORIE courses would show the minor as complete.
- **Suggested change:** Add an 18-credit minimum, for example via a credit-based additional requirement or a checkerWarning noting that the six courses must total at least 18 credits.
