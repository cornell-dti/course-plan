The data file splits the five-course PHIL minor into separate slots: 2 courses at 3000+, 1 history course, 1 course at 2000+, and 1 course at 1000+. This correctly limits students to one 1000-level course, requires two at the 3000 level or higher, and excludes FWS and X9XX courses, as the 2026-2027 catalog says. The differences are mostly in what students are told: one description promises an FWS exception that the checker (and the catalog) do not allow, the 3000-level threshold is worded as "above 3000", and the cap of two courses from other institutions is mentioned but not checked or flagged. The history-before-1900 slot accepts only PHIL 2200 and PHIL 2220, with a checkerWarning saying other courses may count.

## Findings

### 1. Additional Course: 1000 or above (low, description)

- **Catalog:** The five Philosophy courses satisfying the minor may not include any First-Year Writing Seminar (PHIL 1110, PHIL 1111, PHIL 1112). The catalog gives no exception by matriculation term.
- **CoursePlan:** The description says: 'Students who matriculated at Cornell in the fall semester of 2024 (or before) may count one PHIL FWS toward the minor.' The checker beside it rejects every FWS (!courseIsFWS(course)), so a student who relies on the description cannot get an FWS to count.
- **Suggested change:** Remove the FWS grandfathering sentence so the description matches the catalog and the checker. If the exception is confirmed elsewhere, encode it explicitly (for example with a checkerWarning or a migration) instead of describing an exception the checker blocks.

### 2. Courses Above 3000 (low, description)

- **Catalog:** At least two courses at the 3000-level or higher.
- **CoursePlan:** The requirement is named 'Courses Above 3000' and the description says 'At least two must be numbered above 3000'. The checker accepts every non-1xxx/2xxx PHIL course, including 3000-level courses, so the checker itself is correct.
- **Suggested change:** Rename to 'Courses at 3000-level or Higher' and change the description to 'At least two must be at the 3000-level or higher.'

### 3. No more than two courses completed at other institutions (low, rule)

- **Catalog:** No more than two courses completed at other institutions.
- **CoursePlan:** The rule appears only in the description of 'Additional Course: 1000 or above'. No checker or checkerWarning enforces or flags it, so transfer courses could fill all five slots without any warning.
- **Suggested change:** Add a checkerWarning (or a self-check note) reminding students that at most two of the five courses may come from other institutions.

### 4. Five Philosophy Courses (15 credits, minimum 3 credits each) (low, count-or-credits)

- **Catalog:** A minimum of five philosophy courses, minimum of 3 credits each, 15 credits total.
- **CoursePlan:** Courses are counted, not credits. courseMeetsCreditMinimum(course, 3) checks only the course's maximum units, so a variable-credit PHIL course taken for fewer than 3 credits would still count.
- **Suggested change:** Add a note (for example a checkerWarning) that each course must be taken for at least 3 credits, or accept this as a known limitation.
