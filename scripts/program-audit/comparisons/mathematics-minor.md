The Mathematics minor file follows the catalog's overall structure: linear algebra and multivariable calculus prerequisites, then four MATH courses at 3000 or above, with one in algebra, one in analysis and at least one at the 4000 level. Several course lists and rules have drifted from the 2026-2027 catalog. The linear algebra prerequisite does not accept MATH 2310. The analysis list is missing MATH 3270 and adds MATH 3230. The algebra list adds MATH 4315 and MATH 4560. The MATH 4\*\*\* wildcard lets in MATH 4900, MATH 4980 and MATH 4997, which the catalog excludes. The prerequisite grade minimums (B-, or B+ for MATH 2310) are not shown to students anywhere.
