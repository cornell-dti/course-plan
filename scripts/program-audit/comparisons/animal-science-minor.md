# Animal Science (minor)

- Catalog: https://catalog.cornell.edu/programs/animal-science-minor/ (2026-2027 edition)
- Data file: `src/data/minors/animal-sci.ts`
- Checked on 2026-11-13 by `claude-opus-5-5`
- Verdict: **out-of-date**

The data file has one 15-credit requirement that accepts BIOAP 1100 and ANSC 1000–4999 courses, excluding ANSC 49\*\*. It gets the 15-credit total and the Special Studies exclusion roughly right. It is missing the catalog's rule that at least one of the four core courses (BIOAP 1100, ANSC 2400, ANSC 2120, ANSC 2210) must be completed. It also does not mention the limit of 3 credits (1 course) shared with the student's major, or the ban on sharing courses with another minor. Its checker rejects 5000+ level ANSC courses even though the catalog sets no level limit.

## Findings

### 1. Core Animal Science course (high, missing-requirement)

- **Catalog:** Students must complete at least one of the four core Animal Science courses (BIOAP 1100, ANSC 2400, ANSC 2120, ANSC 2210).
- **CoursePlan:** No core requirement exists. The 'Categories' requirement is met by any 15 credits of BIOAP 1100 or ANSC courses, so a student with no core course would see the minor as complete.
- **Suggested change:** Add a requirement 'Core Course' with includesWithSingleRequirement('BIOAP 1100', 'ANSC 2400', 'ANSC 2120', 'ANSC 2210'), fulfilledBy 'courses', perSlotMinCount [1] and allowCourseDoubleCounting: true, so the core course also counts toward the 15 credits.

### 2. Overlap with major / other minors (medium, rule)

- **Catalog:** No more than 3 credits (1 course) can be used to fulfill requirements for the student's major. Credits or courses used for the Animal Science minor cannot be used to meet requirements for another minor.
- **CoursePlan:** Neither rule appears in the description or a checkerWarning, so a student could count several major courses toward the 15 credits and see the minor as complete.
- **Suggested change:** State both overlap limits in the description, or add a checkerWarning telling students to confirm that at most 3 credits (1 course) overlap with their major and none overlap with another minor.

### 3. Categories (medium, course-list)

- **Catalog:** Students must complete animal science courses (ANSC-coded courses, including cross-listed courses and BIOAP 1100). No course-level restriction is stated.
- **CoursePlan:** The checker accepts only ANSC courses numbered 1**_–4_**, so any ANSC 5000+ level course is rejected.
- **Suggested change:** Accept every ANSC catalog number apart from the Special Studies courses, unless the department confirms that graduate-level courses are excluded.

### 4. Categories (low, description)

- **Catalog:** Special Studies courses cannot count towards the total ANSC credit hours; at least one core course is required; overlap limits apply.
- **CoursePlan:** The description only says 'at least 15 credit hours of Animal Science courses'. The source is the CALS department page rather than the catalog.
- **Suggested change:** Expand the description to mention BIOAP 1100, the Special Studies exclusion and the overlap limits. Point the source at https://catalog.cornell.edu/programs/animal-science-minor/.
