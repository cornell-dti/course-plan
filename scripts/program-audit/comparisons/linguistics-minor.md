### 2. Additional Course: 3000 or above / 2000 or above / 1000 or above (medium, course-list)

- **Catalog:** Five courses in Linguistics or courses approved for the linguistics major. Cross-listed courses count whether taken under the LING prefix or another prefix.

### 3. Minor prerequisites (grade) (low, rule)

- **Catalog:** Minimum grade of B- in LING 1101 and in at least one other LING course which counts towards the minor.
- **CoursePlan:** No requirement, description or checkerWarning mentions the B- minimum.
- **Suggested change:** Add a self-check requirement or a checkerWarning noting the B- minimum in LING 1101 and one other LING course counted toward the minor.

### 4. Additional Course: 1000 or above (low, description)

- **Catalog:** No more than one of the three additional courses may be at the 1000 level.
- **CoursePlan:** 'At most 1 additional linguistics course can be 1000 level or above, but it must be at least 3 credits.' This reads as a cap on courses at 1000 level or higher, but the slot actually accepts any LING course of any level except LING 1101.
- **Suggested change:** Reword to something like 'One additional linguistics course of at least 3 credits at any level (this is the only one of the three that may be at the 1000 level). Language courses and FWS do not count.'
