Requirements 1 and 2 match the catalog once cross-listings are applied (ECE 2400 = ENGRD 2140; ECE 3140 = CS 3420). The elective requirement has three problems. It rejects CS 2802, which the catalog accepts alongside CS 2800. It rejects CS 3110, CS 3410 and CS 3420 outright, so a student who takes two of them cannot count the second as an elective. It does not enforce the 18-credit minimum for the minor.

## Findings

### 1. 4 CS courses numbered 3000 or higher (medium, course-list)

- **Catalog:** Additional Courses: select four from CS 2800 or CS 2802, plus CS non-seminar courses at a 3000-level or higher.
- **CoursePlan:** The checker accepts 'CS 2800' but rejects every other CS 2\*\*\* course, so CS 2802 cannot count.
- **Suggested change:** Add 'CS 2802' alongside 'CS 2800' in the checker. Update the description to 'CS 2800 or CS 2802 is allowed'.

### 2. 4 CS courses numbered 3000 or higher (medium, rule)

- **Catalog:** Any CS non-seminar course at 3000-level or higher counts as an additional course, except CS 4090, CS 4997, CS 4998 and CS 4999. A second course from the CS 3110 / CS 3410 / CS 3420 group, beyond the one used for the required course, is a 3000-level CS course and should count.
- **CoursePlan:** The checker always rejects 'CS 3110', 'CS 3410' and 'CS 3420' (marked 'TODO: Temp. excluding courses for Req 2'). A student who takes both CS 3110 and CS 3410 cannot count the second one as an elective.
- **Suggested change:** Remove CS 3110, CS 3410 and CS 3420 from the exclusion list. CoursePlan's no-double-counting rule already stops one course from counting toward both Requirement 2 and the electives.

### 3. Minimum credits for minor (medium, count-or-credits)

- **Catalog:** Minimum Credits for Minor: 18. At least six courses; the Engineering minor rules say 'six courses (at least 18 credits)'.
- **CoursePlan:** Only course counts are enforced (1 + 1 + 4). There is no credit minimum, so six courses that include low-credit CS courses could show the minor as complete with fewer than 18 credits.
- **Suggested change:** Add an 18-credit check for the minor, for example a self-check requirement or a credits-based requirement with allowCourseDoubleCounting across the minor's courses.

### 4. 4 CS courses numbered 3000 or higher (low, description)

- **Catalog:** CS 2800 or CS 2802 may count toward the four additional courses.
- **CoursePlan:** The description reads 'CS 2800 is allowed' and does not mention CS 2802.
- **Suggested change:** Change the description to 'CS 2800 or CS 2802 is allowed'.
