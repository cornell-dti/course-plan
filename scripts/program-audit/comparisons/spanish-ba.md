### 2. Electives (medium, course-list)

### 3. Core Courses (low, rule)

- **Catalog:** The four required courses (SPAN 2140 or SPAN 2235; SPAN 2150 or SPAN 2205; SPAN 2170; SPAN 2180) must be offered by Cornell. No substitutions are permitted.

- **Suggested change:** Add disallowTransferCredit: true. Note in the description that these courses must be taken at Cornell and that no substitutions are permitted.
